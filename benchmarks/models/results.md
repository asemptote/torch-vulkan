# Model benchmarks: eager PyTorch on vk and CUDA

PyTorch's own model suites (TorchBench, TIMM, HuggingFace: `benchmarks/dynamo/` in
the PyTorch repository) in eager mode, on one NVIDIA L40S (driver 615.71), once on
CUDA and once on vk. Method: [docs/log/M51](../../docs/log/M51-model-benchmarks.md);
scripts: this directory. Generated by `report.py` from `sweep.py`'s results.

- **Versions.** torch-vulkan at main 269e49d; PyTorch 2.15.0 (d0f2af8b; CUDA:
  the cu132 build); the suites' pins at that commit: TorchBench c5ea24d7,
  timm af3732ee, transformers 5.13.0.
- **Latency** is the runners' own `abs_latency` (`--nothing --performance`): the
  median of 10 timed calls (30 for the timm float32 rows, 3 for the HF LLMs'
  `generate` calls, which are left out of the geometric means: a generation ends
  at an end-of-sequence token, so the two devices can generate different
  lengths), each synchronised, after the runner's warm-up, at the suites'
  batch sizes. "vk / CUDA" is vk's time over CUDA's: above 1, vk is slower. At
  float32 the runners set `allow_tf32`, so CUDA uses TF32 and vk the "high"
  float32 matrix-product precision (three bfloat16 products, M41).
- **Accuracy** is the runners' accuracy check (`torch._dynamo.utils.same`, their
  tolerance per model) of vk's outputs against CUDA's, with the CPU's float64
  outputs as the error reference, at the runners' accuracy batch sizes, with both
  devices running the same weights and inputs (from a CPU load). "rel. error vs
  fp64" is each device's relative RMS error against those float64 outputs.
- **Runs on vk** means every vk step ran with the CPU fallback off (the default).
  "ops on the CPU" are what went to the CPU with `TORCH_VK_CPU_FALLBACK=1`, with
  the number of calls.
- **Not run:** the 11 detectron2 models (detectron2 not built), DALLE2_pytorch and
  fambench_dlrm / fambench_xlmr (canary models, not installed),
  attention_is_all_you_need_pytorch, pplbench_beanmachine and pytorch_struct (not
  in TorchBench at the pinned commit), meta-llama/Llama-3.2-1B and
  google/gemma-2-2b (gated on the Hub), and the models the runners skip on every
  device (their yaml files' `skip: all`).
- **Left out** of the counts: models that fail on CUDA too (maml_omniglot and
  speech_transformer: TorchBench at its pin is broken on this numpy; the two
  `*_quantized_qat` models: their eval test supports only the CPU), and models
  whose TorchBench benchmark builds them only for cpu and cuda (moco,
  nvidia_deeprecommender).
- **No accuracy comparison** where the CPU load's weights or inputs could not
  be applied to the model loaded on the device (drq, soft_actor_critic, mnasnet1_0,
  Super_SloMo, demucs) or where the reference failed.
- **Issues** for the blockers: #131 (matrix products with more than 65,535
  workgroups along N; fixed on main after this run), #132
  (`scaled_dot_product_attention` materialises the attention matrix), #133
  (device memory allocations fail far below the device's memory; #141), #134
  (16-bit inference slower than float32), #142 (convolution gives wrong results
  when its im2col columns pass 4 GiB: pytorch_unet's accuracy failure), #143
  (`torchvision::nms` and `roi_align`). maml's bfloat16 accuracy failure is the
  runner's 1e-4 tolerance against two different bfloat16 roundings (vk's error
  against float64 is 0.25, CUDA's 0.35), not a vk problem.
